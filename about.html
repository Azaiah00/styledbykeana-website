Natural Hair Artistry

Meet Keana, natural hair artist in Berkeley.

Keana Anjelica Mary is the owner of StyledByKeana. Clients also call her Keke, and her menu for kids 12 and under is Keke Loves the Kids.

About

A salon experience rooted in intention.

StyledByKeana is a Bay Area-based salon experience rooted in intention, care, and transformation.

We specialize in protective styles, precision cuts, natural hair care, and customized services for men, women, and children. Every crown is unique, and here, it’s treated like royalty.

From wash day to silk presses, loc care to braid artistry, we create a space where you feel seen, celebrated, and styled with love.

Mission

“My mission as a stylist is to bring your vision to life with excellence, care, and consistency.”

Every service is done with purpose: to help you fall in love with your look, honor your crown, and leave feeling confident and restored.

Vision

Leave empowered, informed & elevated.

“StyledByKeana will change the way you think and feel about your hair. We take time to educate you on your hair, products, and styling choices so you leave empowered, informed, and elevated.”

Core beliefs

What every appointment stands on.

  • Learning & growth

  • Trust & transparency

  • Creativity & care

  • Flexibility & professionalism

“We create more than styles—we create a safe, empowering space where beauty and community meet.”
