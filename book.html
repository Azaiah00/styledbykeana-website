Policies & etiquette

The fine print, in full.

Read these before you book. They are the same policies listed on Keana’s Booksy page.

  • 15-minute grace period

    Running late? Contact Keana 30 to 60 minutes before your appointment. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean your appointment is canceled or rescheduled with a fee.

  • Braiding hair is not provided

    Please bring your own braiding hair. Any type is welcome.

  • Takedown is not included

    Takedown is a separate service. Book Take down ($75+, about 45 minutes) on its own.

  • Same-day or day-off fee: $75+

    For same-day bookings, or appointments on a day off (typically Monday).

  • Early morning fee: $50+

    For appointments that start before regular hours.

  • Pre-book before you leave

    Repeat clients can pre-book their next appointment before leaving the chair.

Booking FAQ

Before you book.

Still unsure? Call (415) 997-9107.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through Keana’s Booksy page: choose your service, pick an open time and confirm. You can also call (415) 997-9107.

Is there a consultation?

Yes. A consultation is free and takes about 30 minutes. Your final price is confirmed at consultation.

Can I book on a day off or the same day?

Yes, with a $75+ same-day or day-off fee. Days off are typically Monday. Appointments before regular hours carry a $50+ early morning fee.

Do I need to bring my own braiding hair?

Yes. Braiding hair is not provided, so please bring your own. Any type is welcome.

Is takedown included in my braid appointment?

No. Takedown is not included. Book Take down ($75+, about 45 minutes) separately.

Can I book my next appointment before I leave?

Yes. Repeat clients can pre-book their next appointment before leaving.

Are Saturdays always available?

Saturday hours are 12 PM to 6 PM, but some upcoming Saturdays are blocked. Check Booksy for live availability.
