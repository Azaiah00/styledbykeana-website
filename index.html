Good to know

Questions, answered.

More on the booking & policies page.

Do you provide braiding hair?

No. Braiding hair is not provided at StyledByKeana, so please bring your own. Any type of braiding hair is welcome.

How much do knotless braids cost?

Singles and knotless braids start at $225+ for jumbo, $250+ for large, $275+ for medium, $450+ for small and $600+ for extra long (Rapunzel), as listed on Booksy. Bohemian knotless with human hair curls starts at $300+. Your final price is confirmed at your consultation.

Is takedown included?

No. Takedown is not included in braid services. Book a Take down ($75+, about 45 minutes) as a separate service.

What if I am running late?

There is a 15-minute grace period. If you are running late, contact Keana 30 to 60 minutes before your appointment. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean your appointment is canceled or rescheduled with a fee.

Do you do kids’ hair and men’s hair?

Yes. Keke Loves the Kids is the menu for kids 12 and under, starting at $100+. Men’s braids, twists and singles start at $75+, and there is a full loc menu too.

Where is the salon and when is it open?

StyledByKeana is at 1518 San Pablo Avenue, Suite 3, Berkeley, CA 94702. Hours are Wednesday to Friday 11 AM to 7 PM and Saturday 12 PM to 6 PM. Sunday, Monday and Tuesday are closed.
