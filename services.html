Before you book

Policies, plainly.

  • 15-minute grace period

    Running late? Contact Keana 30 to 60 minutes before your appointment. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean your appointment is canceled or rescheduled with a fee.

  • Braiding hair is not provided

    Please bring your own braiding hair. Any type is welcome.

  • Takedown is not included

    Takedown is a separate service. Book Take down ($75+, about 45 minutes) on its own.

  • Same-day or day-off fee: $75+

    For same-day bookings, or appointments on a day off (typically Monday).

  • Early morning fee: $50+

    For appointments that start before regular hours.

  • Pre-book before you leave

    Repeat clients can pre-book their next appointment before leaving the chair.
